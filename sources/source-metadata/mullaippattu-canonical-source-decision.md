# முல்லைப்பாட்டு canonical-source decision

Date: 2026-07-28

## Decision

Project Madurai `pmuni0488` is selected for the fifth Pattuppāṭṭu record. It is not a source-only release.

> The selected Project Madurai object is a commentary-bearing edition. Its literary text is the canonical transcription source for this corpus record, while commentary and editorial prose are separately classified and cannot enter the canonical literary body.

The selected edition is not treated as identical to another Project Madurai edition.

- Editor: உ.வே. சாமிநாத அய்யர்
- Commentator: நச்சினார்க்கினியர்
- Preserved file: `sources/raw-html/pattuppattu/pmuni0488.html`
- SHA-256: `a0d65a61de2f17954d7e46af68e47e554f56633b0bd1f444312e7dde572130a6`
- Coverage: the complete printed literary text appears as one contiguous block of 103 verse lines before the commentary.
- Boundary: the block begins after the printed heading `முல்லைப்பாட்டு` and ends with `வினைவிளங்கு நெடுந்தேர் பூண்ட மாவே.`; numbered commentary starts immediately afterward.
- Advantage: the literary text is independently and reproducibly extractable from the preserved object.
- Limitation: the object is edition- and commentary-bearing. Commentary, variant notes and later prose must remain outside the canonical body.

## Other supplied candidates

No other Mullai source object was supplied or retrieved. Accordingly, no source-only Mullai object is available in the verified ten-object set.

## Isolation policy

The contiguous 103-line literary block is transcribed as canonical text. Printed source prose, commentary, variant readings and editorial notes remain preserved in the raw HTML and are described in the Pattuppāṭṭu apparatus. They are not merged into the poem body or treated as source notes belonging to the poem itself.
