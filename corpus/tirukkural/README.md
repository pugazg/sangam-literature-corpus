# திருக்குறள் (Tirukkural)

Corpus schema version: **1.0.0**  
Version status: **frozen**

This is the source-faithful transcription of Project Madurai `pmuni0001`
([source](https://www.projectmadurai.org/pm_etexts/utf8/pmuni0001.html)),
preserved with SHA-256
`1d1704d1f2e6dd649d40f29253bb6cb1ac8a0e316841601e3b3223feac548f18`.

The corpus contains 1,330 ordered couplet records. It preserves the source's
three major divisions, subordinate headings, and 133 chapter positions.
Chapter 116 has no distinct printed chapter heading; this absence is recorded
instead of repaired. The malformed printed headings `2,3.6` and `3..2. 9`
remain provenance evidence.

Printed line-end numbers are HTML layout markers. They are excluded from the
literary body and retained as `poem_number_as_printed`. Four mismatches
(canonical records 72, 753, 781 and 1293) remain explicit. The repeated work
title before each major division is navigation, not literary text.

`முப்பால்` is an alias of Tirukkural and is not represented as another corpus
work. No commentary is present in the selected source. This freeze certifies
the reproducible transcription layer, not a corrected or critical edition.
