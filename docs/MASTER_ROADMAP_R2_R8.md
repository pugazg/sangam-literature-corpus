# Classical Tamil Research Programme — Master Roadmap R2–R8

## Programme goal

Complete a source-faithful, provenance-bearing and reproducible research programme from the current R2 production boundary through Roadmap 8 for the frozen Classical Tamil Corpus.

This programme was explicitly authorized by the user after Kuṟuntokai R2 production reached record 401. Authorization establishes the complete direction; it does not permit phase skipping or silent weakening of evidence gates.

## Permanent controls

- Corpus 1.1.0 remains immutable.
- Exact source Tamil wins.
- Every production record considers the exact 29 dimensions.
- Reviewed-empty means no qualifying evidence identified, never historical absence.
- Tolkāppiyam never auto-classifies another work.
- Printed names remain unresolved mentions unless a later cited review verifies identity.
- Historical, geographical, biological, community and sectarian equivalence require separate evidence.
- Every roadmap closes with deterministic validation, provenance audit and documentation continuity.
- Draft PRs are not merged without explicit user authorization.

## R2 — Core Sangam production

1. Close Kuṟuntokai completion and exact-head validation.
2. Complete Naṟṟiṇai, Aiṅkuṟunūṟu, Akanāṉūṟu, Kalittokai, Paripāṭal and Patiṟṟuppattu.
3. Complete the ten Pattuppāṭṭu long works independently.
4. Integrate the carried-forward Puṟanāṉūṟu foundation.
5. Prove all 2,376 Core records are represented.
6. Generate unified provenance-bearing views and close the R2 audit.

Each new work begins with a two-record benchmark or, for a one-record long work, an adapter benchmark over the complete work structure.

## R2B — Patiṉeṇkīḻkkaṇakku production

Review all eighteen works independently under the same 29-dimension source-first contract. Each work receives its own folder, metadata adapter, benchmark, contiguous production ledger and completion report. This stage must close before R3 begins.

## R3 — reviewed entities and relationships

- extract mention candidates from completed production ledgers;
- preserve source forms and provenance;
- create append-only review events;
- distinguish same-form, possible-match and verified identity;
- prohibit automatic cross-work identity collapse;
- publish only reviewed relationships.

## R4 — evidence-bounded civilisation and lived-life synthesis

Generate dimension- and work-scoped syntheses only from reviewed observations. Every statement must trace to records and evidence spans. Represent contradiction, uncertainty, genre effects and uneven coverage. Do not present literary evidence as direct census or universal historical fact.

## R5 — research explorer

Build record, work, collection and dimension exploration; search; evidence panels; maps, timelines and networks only where reviewed data supports them. UI summaries must expose provenance and uncertainty rather than hide them.

## R6 — publication and reproducibility hardening

Provide accessible static exports, machine-readable releases, schema/version documentation, checksums, deterministic builds, archival packaging, accessibility review and reproducibility instructions. No new interpretive claims are introduced at this stage.

## R7 — Tolkāppiyam ↔ corpus comparison

Create a separately reviewed comparison layer between Tolkāppiyam formal grammar/poetics evidence and completed literary production. Correspondence, contrast and non-correspondence must be explicit review decisions. No rule automatically classifies a poem.

## R8 — external scholarship and historical identification

Add cited external editions, scholarship, geography, chronology, taxonomy and historical-identification claims as a separate evidence class. Record citation, claim scope, reviewer decision, confidence, contradiction and supersession history. External claims never overwrite canonical source evidence.

## Final completion gate

The programme is complete only when all activated work units and roadmaps have:

- gap-free reviewed ledgers;
- exact source snapshots and evidence spans;
- deterministic materialization;
- schema and regression validation;
- frozen-corpus non-drift proof;
- provenance-complete exports;
- current handover/status documentation;
- an explicit user-authorized merge/release decision.
