The exact 27-இயல் source-sequence ranges are frozen in `research/audits/r15-premerge/tolkappiyam/review-manifest.json`.

## Evidence-role distinction

This audit distinguishes:

- `GRAMMATICAL_CONCEPT_EVIDENCE` — the நூற்பா directly treats the concept as grammar, poetics, semantics or an explicit classified category;
- `INCIDENTAL_EXAMPLE` — a lexical item occurs as an example inside another grammatical rule;
- `NO_QUALIFYING_EVIDENCE` — this நூற்பா does not provide qualifying matrix evidence.

The review manifest proves exhaustive reading coverage. `dimension-crosswalk.json` records representative formal support; it deliberately does **not** pretend to be an exhaustive concordance of every incidental example-word.

## Findings by அதிகாரம்

### Eḻuttatikāram

Its principal matrix contribution is knowledge/technology: phonology, articulation, orthography and grammatical method. It also has formal or example-level material touching body/articulatory anatomy, person/gender, time, number/measure, plants, food and animals. Ecological or material example words are not promoted into cultural claims merely because they appear in a phonological rule.

### Sollatikāram

Its overlap is broader and often formal semantic classification. Examples include:

- `குடிமை, ஆண்மை, இளமை, மூப்பு, அடிமை, வன்மை, விருந்து, குழு, பெண்மை, அரசு, மகவு, குழவி, காதல்`;
- `காலம், உலகம், உயிர், உடம்பு, தெய்வம், வினை, பூதம், ஞாயிறு, திங்கள்`;
- grammatical/semantic treatment of gender, kinship, social grouping, occupation, time, place, emotion, illness, poverty, fear, desire, praise/blame and regional/social language use.

These remain linguistic/semantic classifications, not automatically historical descriptions of a population.

### Poruḷatikāram

This is the strongest direct indigenous framework for the matrix.

`அகத்திணையியல்` formally defines seven tiṇai; `முதல்` as land/time; relational-emotional `உரிப்பொருள்`; and `கரு` categories including deity, food, fauna, tree/flora, bird, drum/activity and yāḻ. It also treats social actors, travel/separation and relational situations.

`புறத்திணையியல்` formally treats polity, cattle raid, military movement, fort/siege, weapons, ruler/land, Korravai/Velan ritual, hero-stone selection/water rite/installation, gifts, camp life, assemblies, performers, omens, wounds, ghosts, widowhood, loss, lament and impermanence.

`களவியல்` and `கற்பியல்` systematize love, union, gendered roles, friend/mother/nurse/father/kin, marriage, household/community norms, public/private rendezvous, hospitality, social reputation, travel, ritual, values and relationship transitions.

`பொருளியல்` and `மெய்ப்பாட்டியல்` formally connect emotion with body and lived experience: disease, ageing, hunger, pallor, reduced eating, bodily wasting, sleep loss, poverty, fear, joy, anger, grief, education, fame, gift, wealth and status.

`செய்யுளியல்` names poem components including tiṇai, speaker, listener, place, time, benefit, emotion, prior context, meaning and tuṟai, and later treats textual forms and textual organization.

`மரபியல்` gives extensive animal/sex/age naming, sensory cognition, role-linked objects for Brahmins/rulers, weapons/transport, commerce (`வாணிக வாழ்க்கை`), food, plough-based agriculture, plant parts, elements, and an explicit theory of first/derivative texts, translation, commentary, examples and scholarly procedure.

## Matrix conclusion

All 29 requested dimensions have some **formal or structural support** in Tolkāppiyam, but not at equal depth:

- `SYSTEMATIC_FORMAL_FRAMEWORK`: 17 dimensions
- `EXPLICIT_FORMAL_SUPPORT`: 11 dimensions
- `FORMAL_SCOPE_LIMITED`: 1 dimension (`named_entities`)

Named entities are deliberately scope-limited: Tolkāppiyam gives name-use rules and named deity labels but is not a historical entity-resolution catalogue.

Most importantly, this crosswalk is **not an automatic classifier**. Tolkāppiyam concept evidence may support later comparison with Sangam poems only through explicit provenance and review.
