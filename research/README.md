# Research layer

This directory contains independently versioned, reproducible derivatives of the immutable Classical Tamil Corpus. It must never be used to edit, normalise, correct, or supersede canonical transcription.

## Version boundary

- Puṟanāṉūṟu R0 source evidence remains research evidence schema `0.1.0`.
- R1 review and entity-resolution workflow remains schema `0.2.0`.
- R1.5 concept observations and concept-matrix infrastructure use schema `0.3.0`.
- The R0 assertion IDs, evidence spans, canonical hashes, printed forms, 2,867-assertion inventory, 285 literary-body candidates, 43 pilot entities, and 51 pilot relationships remain the compatibility baseline.
- R1/R1.5 operate on the current Corpus 1.1.0 tree without modifying canonical corpus evidence or Tolkāppiyam.

## Directory roles

- `schemas/`: reusable R0 evidence schemas, R1 workflow schemas, and R1.5 concept-observation/Tolkāppiyam concept-evidence schemas.
- `controlled-vocabularies/`: versioned evidence, review, reviewer, entity-resolution, classification-basis, concept-dimension, and concept-evidence-policy vocabularies.
- `evidence/purananuru/`: authoritative deterministic R0 assertions and reporting CSV.
- `mentions/purananuru/`: unresolved R0 literary-body mention candidates.
- `entities/pilot/`: R0 surface-form entity sample plus append-only R1 entity-resolution decisions.
- `relationships/pilot/`: assertion-supported R0 pilot relationships.
- `reviews/purananuru/`: append-only review events plus deterministic review queues/exports.
- `concepts/classical-tamil/`: versioned R1.5 concept registry and concept hierarchy.
- `pilots/purananuru/`: bounded R1.5 assertion-to-concept mapping contract.
- `observations/purananuru/`: deterministic R1.5 provenance-bearing concept observations.
- `observations/tolkappiyam/`: separately governed grammatical/poetics concept-evidence stream; R1.5 defines the contract but intentionally contains no production NDJSON observations.
- `matrices/purananuru/`: deterministic long-form matrix views derived from concept observations.
- `reports/`: coverage, ambiguity, review, unresolved-entity, and R1.5 pilot reports.

## Primary histories versus generated views

`reviews/purananuru/review-events.ndjson` and `entities/pilot/entity-resolution-decisions.ndjson` are primary histories. They are never regenerated from scratch or silently truncated. Review events are ordered and hash-chained; entity decisions remain assertion-provenanced and auditable.

R1 review queues/exports and R1.5 Puṟanāṉūṟu observations/matrices/summaries are deterministic derived views. They may be regenerated byte-for-byte from their declared primary inputs and frozen evidence.

## Review and identity semantics

`machine_checked` is not human verification. `reviewed` requires an explicit review event, and `verified` requires a stronger explicit verification decision. Assistant-assisted review is identified as `assistant_assisted` and does not establish a verified historical identity by itself.

`possible_match` / `POSSIBLY_SAME_AS` remains distinct from verified identity. Exact printed-form equality or lookup-normalised equality never causes an automatic entity merge.

R1.5 concept membership is also separate from historical identity. A generic ruler, place, polity, community, deity, flora/fauna, occupation, or other concept observation does not by itself establish a modern historical identification.

## Matrix semantics

A populated matrix row is a view over one or more provenance-bearing observations. It retains assertion/evidence traceability, classification basis, evidence class, confidence, and review state. It is not a yes/no historical fact flag.

An empty cell means only **no qualifying evidence is currently recorded**. It must never be interpreted as evidence that the feature was historically absent.

The concept registry provides foundation families for Akam/Puram, tiṇai, tuṟai, the five classical landscape families, named entities, and lived-life dimensions. Conventional associations between these families are not automatically asserted; each populated association requires its own evidence.

## Tolkāppiyam concept stream

Tolkāppiyam grammatical/poetics concepts use a separate evidence contract with `GRAMMATICAL_CONCEPT_EVIDENCE` and `tolkappiyam_mapping`. R1.5 establishes that schema and boundary only. It intentionally creates **zero** production Tolkāppiyam concept observations and does not treat a நூற்பா as a poem-level classification.

Future cross-text use must preserve the difference between Tolkāppiyam grammatical evidence and poem-level source evidence.

## Regeneration and validation

```bash
python3 scripts/generate_research_layer.py --root .
python3 scripts/generate_research_r1.py --root .
python3 scripts/generate_research_r15.py --root .

python3 scripts/validate_research_layer.py --root .
python3 scripts/validate_research_r1.py --root .
python3 scripts/validate_research_r15.py --root .
python3 scripts/validate_research_r15_acceptance.py --root .

python3 scripts/verify_research_r1_idempotence.py --root .
python3 scripts/verify_research_r15_idempotence.py --root .
python3 scripts/create_research_r15_baseline.py --root .

pytest -q
python3 scripts/audit_repository.py --root .
```

The R0 compatibility wrapper preserves existing append-only review history while delegating all R0 evidence generation to the preserved R0 implementation.

All deterministic generated content excludes execution timestamps. Human/editorial review must append review events rather than mutating assertion history. R1.5 contains no populated external-historical or interpretive observations and no verified historical identity. R2 may begin only after R1.5 validation, deterministic regeneration, full regression tests, repository audit, frozen-corpus non-drift, continuity handover, and merge acceptance are complete.
