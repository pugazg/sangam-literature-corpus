# Nāṉmaṇikkaṭigai architecture review

Project Madurai `pmuni0047` numbers the invocation as record 1 and continues
through record 106. The canonical unit is therefore 106 source-numbered verses,
not an assumed round count. The two printed headings `கடவுள் வாழ்த்து` and
`நூல்` supply the section strategy. Line-end numbers are layout labels retained
in provenance and excluded from literary bodies.
