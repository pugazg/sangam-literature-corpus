## Source conditions retained

- Aintiṇai Eḻupathu records 25, 26, 69, and 70 are source-lost.
- Tiṇaimālai prints 153 numbered records despite its nominal title count.
- Tirikaṭukam lacks printed headings 43 and 57.
- Ācārakkōvai lacks heading 47 and retains anomalous heading punctuation.
- Paḻamoḻi prints 399 numbered records plus two unnumbered opening texts and
  omits chapter ordinal 12.
- Kainnilai prints only four tiṇai headings for its 60 records; no fifth
  heading was supplied editorially.
- Printed variants, prose notes, ellipses, repeated openings, and unnumbered
  closing texts remain explicit and were not silently corrected or promoted.
