# Nālāṭiyār architecture review

The common preservation, schema, manifest, auditing and fidelity layers are
reused. A source-specific `pmuni0016` parser is required because record numbers
are prefixes on the first verse line, an unnumbered invocation precedes the
numbered corpus, and forty printed chapter headings are nested beneath three
major divisions.

Canonical units are the 400 printed numbered quatrains. The invocation is
preserved at work level. Forty section files mirror printed chapter positions;
they are not arbitrary navigation ranges. The printed `2.39` chapter heading
under `3. காமத்துப்பால்` remains unchanged in provenance.
