# Patiṉeṇkīḻkkaṇakku final-eight architecture review

The shared preservation, schema, physical-audit, manifest, fidelity and
idempotence layers were retained. Explicit parser profiles were added for each
observed source grammar; there is no fallback parser.

- Tiṇaimoḻi Aimpathu: terminal line-end numbers and five printed tiṇai headings.
- Tirikaṭukam: four-line records, an unnumbered காப்பு, and absent printed
  record headings 43 and 57.
- Ācārakkōvai: metre labels delimit variable-length verse records; record 47
  lacks a printed heading and punctuation anomalies remain as printed.
- Paḻamoḻi Nāṉūṟu: 399 printed numbered records, two unnumbered opening texts,
  and 33 printed chapter headings with ordinal 12 absent.
- Ciṟupañcamūlam: terminal line-end numbering 1–98; the invocation and final
  பாயிரம் are numbered by the selected source.
- Mutumoḻik Kāñci: 100 records in ten printed பத்து groups.
- Ēlāti: two unnumbered prefatory texts and 80 terminal-numbered records.
- Kainnilai: 60 records and printed துறை notes; only four tiṇai headings are
  present, so no fifth heading is inferred.

Mechanical sections are labelled as navigation. Source headings generate
sections only where they are actually printed. Prose is never mapped to a
controlled speaker value. Printed ellipses and irregular punctuation remain
canonical evidence.
